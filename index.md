# Hi, I'm Noga!

**Scientist by day, climber whenever possible.**

## About Me

I'm a PhD student in [Prof. Moran Shalev-Benami's lab](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/csb/shalev-benami/) at the Weizmann Institute of Science.

## THE SCIENCE SIDE

I study microbial rhodopsins, a family of light-sensitive proteins.

My research focuses on understanding how these proteins work, how they respond to light, and how their structure relates to their function.

To study these proteins, I use cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM), a technique that allows us to visualize their structures at near-atomic resolution.

## BEYOND THE LAB

When I'm not in the lab, you'll probably find me hanging off a rock somewhere.

I love rock climbing, traveling, and spending time outdoors.
